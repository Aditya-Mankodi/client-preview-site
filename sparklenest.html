FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking with SparkleNest.

How do I book a cleaning with SparkleNest?
The easiest way is to message us directly on WhatsApp. You can also fill out our contact form and we'll get back to you within a few hours to confirm your booking.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
Not necessarily. Many of our clients provide access and we clean while they're at work. We'll discuss the best arrangement for you when booking.
What areas in Dubai do you cover?
We currently serve Dubai Marina, JVC, Downtown Dubai, JLT, and Business Bay. If you're in a nearby area, reach out and we'll do our best to accommodate you.
Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?
Yes — our team arrives fully equipped with eco-friendly, professional-grade cleaning products and all the equipment needed to get the job done.
Are your cleaners trustworthy and background-checked?
Absolutely. All SparkleNest team members go through a thorough vetting and background-check process before they're approved to work in client homes.
What if I'm not satisfied with the clean?
We stand by our work. If you're not fully satisfied, let us know within 24 hours and we'll return to address any issues at no additional charge.
